The white door opens inward, onto a kitchen. Your kitchen. The kettle is just coming to the boil.

  
The light is the ordinary kind. The clock on the wall shows the same minute you leaned on that wall in the corridor.

  
You sit down at the table. Nobody has noticed you were gone. You are no longer sure that you were.

  
On the table is a cassette recorder you have never owned. Its red light is on. It is still recording.

  
When at last you look back, the door is only the pantry door. Behind it, very faintly, something is humming.
